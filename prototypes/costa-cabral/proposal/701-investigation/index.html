How this evidence is used

Full service → locate added delay or a model defect → reduce it to a short native test → test one change → rerun full service with the same requests. Reject collisions, missing services and unserved demand. Exact-second and 60-second results remain separate.

The street geometry comes from the frozen OSM import. Signal programmes and demand remain assumptions. The operator timetable is unchanged. Full-day combined passenger/traffic qualification and field validation remain required.
